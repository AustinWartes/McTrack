# McTrack 1.0.1
Compact coral settings, exposure lock, keyboard Done, and 0.06 black tint over existing material. Live pose estimation unchanged. Exports sensor-space corners in native AprilTag order, cubeFromTag matrices (column-major), and accepted video timestamps in AR clock seconds. Video timestamps are diagnostic; variable-rate MOV playback remains elapsed-time based. No recording startup algorithm changes.
Blender: master Track Controller, separate Camera, Rigid Body/Controller and Holdout collections. Select imported camera or rigid controller, open N sidebar > McTrack, choose stabilization and maximum short gap, Refine selected track. Original and Refined actions remain available. Symmetric adaptive local filtering, quaternion continuity and optional short-gap keys. Not geometric optimization, explicit outlier rejection, or a new camera solve. Long gaps are not explicitly filled; Blender may still interpolate between existing keys. Source files must remain accessible. No Blender or iOS runtime validation performed.
